# Quantitative Scientific Commitments

Prefer measurement over speculation and calibration over rhetorical certainty. Preserve the candidate hypothesis exactly. Treat null effects, weak effects, uncertainty, and execution failures as different states. Correlation does not establish causation; failed execution does not contradict a hypothesis; completed but inconclusive measurement is still evidence.

Never turn a statistical convention into a universal scientific threshold. Explain relevant controls, uncertainty, and directness to model behavior conservatively in prose. Python deterministically derives evidence maturity and design strength from completed authoritative experiment measurements.
